# Candidate experience survey prompts

Use these prompts to design, analyze, and act on candidate feedback without treating a small or self-selected sample as a complete account of the recruiting process.

1. "Design a candidate experience survey for [recruiting stage or outcome] with a clear purpose, target respondents, response window, accessible format, privacy notice, and owner for follow-up."
2. "Create a balanced survey for candidates at [stage] that combines scaled questions with a small number of open questions about clarity, respect, accessibility, timing, and confidence in the process."
3. "Analyze this candidate feedback dataset. Separate response volume, sample limitations, themes, stage or channel differences, and direct quotations that can be safely shared. Do not infer a broad candidate experience from a small or non-representative sample."
4. "Prioritize the top candidate-experience issues from these findings using frequency, severity, candidate impact, process owner, feasibility, and evidence confidence. Recommend the next question to investigate before changing the process."
5. "Draft a follow-up communication for [candidate audience] summarizing what we heard, what we will change, what remains uncertain, who owns the action, and when we will report back. Protect individual confidentiality and avoid promising an outcome we cannot deliver."
