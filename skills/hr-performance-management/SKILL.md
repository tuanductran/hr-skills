# HR performance management

End-to-end performance management support — from goal setting and performance reviews to 360-degree feedback, performance improvement plans, recognition programs, and succession planning.

## Supported tasks

- Writing performance reviews and appraisal reports
- Creating performance improvement plans (PIPs)
- Designing performance appraisal forms and templates
- Setting employee goals and OKRs
- Conducting 360-degree feedback assessments
- Developing performance metrics and KPIs
- Running performance calibration sessions
- Designing employee recognition and awards programs
- Writing talent review reports
- Developing succession planning programs

## Key prompts

### Performance reviews

1. "Write a performance review for [employee name] in the [job title] role, focusing on their achievements in [specific areas] over [time period]."
2. "What are the essential elements of an effective performance review template for [department/role]?"
3. "How should I structure a performance review conversation to be constructive and forward-looking?"
4. "What phrasing and language should I use in performance reviews to deliver clear, actionable feedback?"
5. "How can I write a performance review summary that accurately captures [employee name]'s contributions and growth opportunities?"
6. "What are best practices for conducting performance review calibration sessions across departments?"

### Performance improvement plans

1. "Help me write a performance improvement plan (PIP) for [employee name] in [department/role], who is struggling with [performance issue]. Include measurable goals for [timeframe]."
2. "What are effective strategies for setting achievable milestones in a PIP for an employee struggling with [specific area]?"
3. "How should I phrase feedback in a PIP to encourage [employee name] to take ownership of their performance improvement?"
4. "How should I structure a PIP document to make expectations clear and objective for [employee name]?"
5. "What are common roadblocks during a PIP, and how can managers help employees overcome them?"
6. "How should I conduct the initial PIP meeting with [employee name] to maintain a supportive yet firm tone?"

### Goal setting

1. "Help me develop SMART performance goals for [employee name] in the [job title] role for [time period]."
2. "What are effective strategies for aligning individual goals with broader company OKRs?"
3. "How can managers collaborate with employees to co-create meaningful and motivating goals?"
4. "What goal-setting frameworks work best for [department/role] teams?"
5. "How can I cascade organizational priorities down to individual contributor performance goals effectively?"

### 360-Degree feedback

1. "Design a 360-degree feedback survey for [role/level] that evaluates [core competencies]."
2. "What questions should be included in a 360-degree feedback assessment for a [management level/role]?"
3. "How should I facilitate a 360-degree feedback debrief session with [employee name]?"
4. "How can I ensure 360-degree feedback is delivered constructively to drive professional development?"
5. "What are best practices for conducting performance gap analysis using 360-degree feedback results?"

### Performance metrics and analytics

1. "What key performance indicators (KPIs) should I track to measure [department/team] effectiveness?"
2. "Help me design a performance tracking dashboard for [department] to monitor key metrics over [time period]."
3. "How can I build a balanced performance scorecard aligned with strategic business objectives?"
4. "What performance and talent metrics should be highlighted in a review report for executive leadership?"
5. "How can I leverage performance evaluation data to identify high-potential employees for succession pipelines?"

### Recognition and succession planning

1. "Design an employee recognition program for [department/company] that aligns with [company values]."
2. "What non-monetary recognition strategies effectively boost morale and engagement for [team type]?"
3. "How can I set up a peer-to-peer recognition program that fosters a continuous culture of appreciation?"
4. "How can I build a succession planning framework to identify and prepare high-potential talent for key roles?"
5. "What criteria and evaluation matrices should be used to assess succession readiness for [leadership role]?"
6. "Write a succession planning framework for [department] that ensures leadership continuity."
7. "How can I build individual development plans (IDPs) tailored for succession candidates in [department]?"

## Tips

- Drive continuous dialog, not annual tick-box reviews. Quarterly check-ins and real-time feedback prevent surprises, improve goal responsiveness, and produce far richer evidence for annual performance assessments.
- Separate performance reviews from compensation discussions. Combining rating delivery with salary decisions shifts employee focus away from feedback and development onto financial outcomes. Hold evaluation and compensation meetings separately.
- Support ratings with objective behavioral evidence. Require concrete metrics, project deliverables, and specific behavioral examples to support ratings. This minimizes manager bias, subjective evaluations, and potential dispute claims.
- Calibrate across teams before finalizing ratings. Conduct calibration sessions with peer managers to align rating standards across departments, mitigate lenient or harsh grading tendencies, and maintain organizational fairness.
- Establish clear, time-bound PIP milestones. Performance Improvement Plans must detail specific gap areas, expected outcomes, support resources, and 30/60/90-day evaluation checkpoints to provide genuinely supportive remediation.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation across all performance stages — informal feedback and verbal warnings lack legal standing in strict labor law jurisdictions like Vietnam.
